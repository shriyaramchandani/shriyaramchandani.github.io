Insight

People were building carts to estimate how much they could save.

While investigating drop-offs in the dining funnel, we found users entering different bill values to calculate their savings. They would try ₹5,000, ₹10,000 or ₹15,000, inspect the total, and leave.

Without making a booking, checkout only showed them a part of the savings.

Restaurants have walk-in and booking offers. Booking offers generally give better discounts, but only appear in the checkout calculation after a booking is made.

Walk-in offers

Included in their calculation

Booking offers

Excluded in their calculation

Users saw 10% savings even when a 20% booking offer was available.

The opportunity

Show savings before checkout.

We decided to help users estimate savings on the restaurant page, using offers available for their date and time, before they enter checkout.

Exploration 01

Show the savings amount

We started with showing the maximum savings amount upfront to get the user’s attention.

From quick user testing

The savings estimate looked like another offer.

We showed the designs to people around the office. They mistook the highlighted savings for an extra discount.

Exploration 02

Remove the number?

The savings amount is what users are looking for. We rejected these ideas because we wanted to show it upfront, without requiring an extra step.

Exploration 03

Change the visual style?

We tested different colours, bill graphics and wallet graphics. People around the office still interpreted the savings amount as a promotional offer.

Exploration 04

Make it look like a bill?

We showed the estimated bill, offer deductions and amount to pay in an itemised layout. We added the label “Sample bill” and the date, meal and party size to explain the estimate.

The final direction

The final design

The final design shows an estimated bill for two, the combined value of eligible offers and the amount the user may pay. The restaurant-bill structure separates the calculation from the promotional offers above it.

  • A useful estimate is visible without extra input.
  • Date, time and party size explain the calculation context.
  • The full sample bill reveals the individual offer breakdown.
